# Availability Zones

## Definition

Availability Zones are physically separate datacenters within an Azure region.

## Purpose

Protect workloads against datacenter failures.

## Example

Norway East
├── Zone 1
├── Zone 2
└── Zone 3

## Benefits

- High availability
- Fault isolation
- Reduced downtime

## Architect Rule

Datacenter failure → Availability Zones

Regional failure → Multiple Regions
